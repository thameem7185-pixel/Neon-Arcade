How To Play

  • Tap or Click any card to flip it over.
  • Flip two cards per turn. If they match, they stay illuminated!
  • If they don't match, memorize their positions before they flip back.
  • Keyboard: Use Arrow Keys to move, Enter or Space to flip.
  • Pause: Hit Esc anytime to pause the timer and hide the cards.
